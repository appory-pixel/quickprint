Quick Print Support

Quick Print lets you print photos, documents, scans, web pages and text from your iPhone or iPad to any AirPrint printer.

Contact us

Email support.appory@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and printer model. We usually reply within two business days.

My printer doesn't appear

Make sure the printer is switched on and awake, supports AirPrint, and is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone or iPad. Guest networks often block printing. Restarting the printer and your router fixes most cases.

The print is cut off or too small

Check the paper size in the print options. For photos, try turning "Fill the space" on or off.

A document looks different on paper

Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are laid out by iOS and can differ slightly from the original. Exporting the file as PDF first gives the most exact result.

Subscriptions

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. To cancel, open Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device at least 24 hours before the renewal date. To restore a subscription on a new device, open Quick Print, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy choices

You can change your analytics and advertising choices at any time in Quick Print under Settings > Privacy choices.
