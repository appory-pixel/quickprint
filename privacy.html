Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Quick Print is provided by Nguyen Le Bui Thao. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Photos, documents and scans are processed on your device. We use RevenueCat to record subscription transactions and measure the performance of Apple Ads campaigns. No account registration is required. Optional Google ad measurement uses advertising identifiers only when you allow both our optional measurement choice and Apple’s tracking request. Personalised advertising (remarketing) is used only if you separately choose it.

Photos, files and scans

Photos, documents and scanned pages that you choose are processed only on your device so that they can be previewed and printed. They are never uploaded to us. Temporary copies made for printing are kept in the app's temporary storage, which iOS clears automatically.

Camera

The camera is used only when you scan a document. Scanned pages stay on your device unless you print or share them yourself.

Printing

Print jobs are sent from your device straight to the printer you select, using Apple's AirPrint. We never see what you print.

Web pages

When you enter a web address, the page is loaded directly from that website. The website may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not record the addresses you visit.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information, a randomly generated app user identifier, and technical information needed to provide its service. We also send Apple’s AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so it can request Apple Ads attribution data from Apple and associate that data with subscription revenue. If you allow the system tracking permission request, Apple may provide more detailed attribution, including when you interacted with an ad. If you decline, only standard attribution is requested. Your choice does not affect printing, Premium access or purchases. You can change your permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. These services do not receive your photos, documents, scans or payment card details. For RevenueCat’s data practices, see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. To ask about this processing or request deletion of data held for this app, contact support.appory@gmail.com.

Optional Google Analytics and ad measurement

If you choose App analytics in Privacy choices, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app activity (such as onboarding steps, the printer brand you pick, which print features you use, paywall views, purchase outcomes and error codes), purchase events, an app instance identifier, and information about your device and app. Google may use IP addresses to derive approximate location. We do not send your photos, documents, scans, file names, web addresses, typed text, names or email addresses to Google. We have disabled collection of the identifier for vendor (IDFV).

If you also choose Measure our Google ads and allow Apple’s tracking request, Google may use advertising identifiers and event data to measure our advertising. If you also choose Personalised ads, Google may use this data to build advertising audiences and show you ads for Quick Print in other apps and on websites (remarketing). Declining optional data does not affect printing, Premium access, or purchases. You can change or withdraw these choices in Settings > Privacy choices, and manage Apple’s permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Data processing is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s partner-site information. Contact support.appory@gmail.com for data requests relating to this app.

Settings

Your preferences (such as default layout and text size) are stored only on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not ask children to provide contact information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and at the policy web address, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support.appory@gmail.com
